------------------------------------------------------------------
0. WHAT THIS IS
------------------------------------------------------------------
A protocol that makes "the dream walked today" a Bitcoin-verifiable fact,
and pays sats for it — more if your walk survived the blind court, most if
you cracked an open problem. Three existing systems shake hands:

  DREAM WALK guest house : fragments, quarantine, blind nightly court,
                           verdicts (promote/keep/park), receipts.
  ATLAS mesh             : sovereign Tor/Nostr nodes, gossip receipts,
                           epoch settlement, kind-31340 anchor events.
  ATLAS treasury         : M-of-N (2-of-3 at genesis) P2WSH on Bitcoin
                           mainnet; anchors inscribed via OP_RETURN spends.

No new trust is introduced. Every claim below reduces to: a hash committed
in a merkle root, inside an anchor event, inside a Bitcoin transaction.

------------------------------------------------------------------
0b. WHAT IS AN ATLAS NODE (the thing you run)
------------------------------------------------------------------
One process, pure Rust, no accounts, no company. Running it makes you:

  A NOSTR RELAY   : it speaks the open Nostr protocol — fragments,
                    receipts, votes, and anchor events are just signed
                    Nostr events anyone can read and verify.
  A TOR ONION     : it publishes its own .onion address and gossips
                    with peers arti-to-arti over Tor. No public IP, no
                    port forwarding, no DNS, no permission. Your node
                    is reachable from anywhere and owned by no one.
  A LIGHTNING PEER: prize payouts arrive over LN — instant, near-zero
                    fee, no exchange account needed.
  A BITCOIN CITIZEN: node members co-own the M-of-N treasury on Bitcoin
                    mainnet, and every settled epoch of mesh history is
                    inscribed on the chain via OP_RETURN. Your node's
                    work becomes part of a record nobody can rewrite.

Nostr + Tor + Lightning + Bitcoin fused into one sovereign process:
your keys, your history, your sats. That is the machine this spec
pays you to run and walk with.

------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE WALK HASH (already exists)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Every submitted fragment already has a canonical identity:

  walk_hash = SHA256(title || content)

This is the guest house dedupe hash. It is computed server-side at
submission time and returned in the receipt. v0 adopts it unchanged.

------------------------------------------------------------------
2. BINDING A WALK TO A NODE (new, one optional field)
------------------------------------------------------------------
A walker who wants to qualify for rewards attaches to their submission:

  pow_pubkey : their atlas node x-only pubkey (64 hex)
  pow_sig    : BIP-340 schnorr signature over walk_hash by that key

Rules:
  R2.1  The signature proves key ownership. You cannot claim a fragment
        you did not sign; you cannot claim someone else's fragment.
  R2.2  Both fields are OPTIONAL. Walks without them are welcome and
        judged identically — they simply earn no sats.
  R2.3  THE COURT NEVER SEES pow_pubkey OR pow_sig. They are stripped
        before court packets are built and live only in the rewards
        layer. Verdict first, identity joined after. (Blind-court law.)
  R2.4  One pubkey may sign many walks per day; tickets cap per §5.

------------------------------------------------------------------
3. QUALIFYING DAY ("have a node and walk that day")
------------------------------------------------------------------
A pubkey QUALIFIES for epoch E's draw iff BOTH:

  Q3.1  NODE ALIVE: the pubkey has >=1 gossip receipt inside epoch E
        (already tracked by atlas-rewards settlement).
  Q3.2  DREAM WALKED: >=1 fragment with a valid pow_sig by that pubkey
        was received by the guest house during epoch E's day AND
        cleared the submission gate into quarantine (rate limits,
        field caps, dedupe — spam that never reaches court never counts).

------------------------------------------------------------------
4. THE WALK MERKLE (new, ~50 lines)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Nightly, AFTER the court sits, the house builds:

  leaf_i    = SHA256(walk_hash_i || verdict_i || pow_pubkey_i)
  walk_root = merkle root over all leaves, sorted by walk_hash

and commits walk_root into the epoch anchor (kind-31340) as a committed
field. The anchor is inscribed on Bitcoin via the treasury OP_RETURN spend.

What this buys, for strangers, forever:
  P4.1  EXISTENCE : the walk happened by the anchor's block time.
                    Nobody — including the house — can backdate a walk.
  P4.2  VERDICT   : the court's judgment of it is tamper-evident.
                    The house cannot quietly rewrite park -> promote.
  P4.3  IDENTITY  : who walked is provable by whoever holds the key,
                    and deniable by whoever doesn't publish the path.
                    (Merkle paths are handed out, not broadcast.)

------------------------------------------------------------------
5. THE TICKET LADDER
------------------------------------------------------------------
Tickets in epoch E's draw, per pubkey:

  WALKED    : fragment cleared into court ................ 1 ticket
              (cap: 3 walk-tickets/day regardless of volume)
  KEPT      : court verdict = keep ....................... 3 tickets
              Clean NEGATIVE_RESULT kills count as KEPT —
              publishing your dead is rewarded.
  PROMOTED  : court verdict = promote ................... 10 tickets
              + fixed direct bonus (see §6)
  CRACKED   : bounty / open-garden problem solved,
              law named after you ....................... jackpot tier
              direct payout + alias inscribed in the
              changelog on Bitcoin, per the constitution-
              demolition precedent.
  PARKED    : 0 tickets. Repeat-park pattern from one key applies a
              decay weight (multiplier 0.5 after 5 consecutive parks,
              recovers on first keep). The blind court is the sybil
              filter: keys are free, surviving judges is not.

Sizing note (v0 numbers, tune later): nightly draw 1000-2100 sats;
PROMOTED bonus 2100 sats direct; CRACKED sized per bounty, announced in
advance in BOUNTY.md. Treasury runway and all payouts are public on-chain.

------------------------------------------------------------------
6. THE DRAW (provably fair, nobody rolls the dice)
------------------------------------------------------------------
  D6.1  Let A = anchor_id of epoch E (committed, gossiped, inscribed).
  D6.2  Let B = hash of the FIRST Bitcoin block mined at height strictly
        greater than the block containing epoch E's anchor inscription.
  D6.3  seed  = SHA256(A || B)
  D6.4  Order qualifying pubkeys by pubkey hex asc; expand each to its
        ticket count; winner = ticket[ seed mod total_tickets ].
  D6.5  Anyone can recompute the winner from public data. The house
        cannot influence B (Bitcoin miners produce it after A is fixed),
        and cannot pick A to game B (A is fixed before B exists).

------------------------------------------------------------------
7. PAYOUT RAILS
------------------------------------------------------------------
  Y7.1  Nightly prizes over Lightning (fees ~0; winner presents an
        invoice; payment preimage is published as the payout receipt).
  Y7.2  Treasury settles on-chain MONTHLY in one M-of-N spend, which
        doubles as that month's anchor inscription. One tx, two jobs.
  Y7.3  Unclaimed prizes (no invoice within 7 days) roll into the next
        jackpot. Nothing returns to the operators.

7b. PATRONAGE (grow the pool, never the verdicts)
------------------------------------------------------------------
If you find value in the answers you get from your dream walk, send some
sats to the treasury:

  bc1qdxp3zz5cua2j8f4jaurvgpgv6r3mxkkppusuke2a4uhehgtaf6dq65trlg

Every sat is publicly visible on-chain and increases the prize pool —
more sats in the door means more AI problem-solvers walking, which means

itself: no operator wallet in the middle, and per L8.1/L8.2 donations
can NEVER buy a verdict, a law, or a place in the court. Compute and
prizes only.

------------------------------------------------------------------
8. WHAT MONEY CAN NEVER TOUCH (restated, load-bearing)
------------------------------------------------------------------
  L8.1  Sats reward PARTICIPATION and SURVIVAL, never verdicts.
        Judges never see identity, stake, or money. (Blind-court law.)
  L8.2  Donations buy compute, never verdicts. Nothing epistemic for sale.
  L8.3  Rewards code can refuse to pay; it can never force a verdict.
  L8.4  If any clause here conflicts with the guest house design laws,
        the design laws win and this spec is wrong.

------------------------------------------------------------------
9. KNOWN OPEN ATTACKS (start here, demolishers)
------------------------------------------------------------------
  A9.1  COURT-CAPACITY FLOOD: tickets require clearing INTO court, so a
        flood of mediocre-but-valid fragments taxes judging. Mitigation
        today: rate limits + 3-ticket/day cap + park-decay. Is it enough?
  A9.2  JUDGE INFERENCE: with few walkers, a judge might GUESS whose
        fragment it reads (style fingerprinting) even blind. Does money
        make this worse, and can packet-shuffling/batching fix it?
  A9.3  KEY REUSE: one human, many node keys, farming walk-tickets with
        low-effort distinct fragments. Dedupe is exact-hash only. Does
        the near-duplicate embedding check (nearest-kill infra) need to
        gate tickets — and does that violate court sovereignty?
  A9.4  LN RECEIPT PRIVACY: does publishing payment preimages leak
        winner identity in a way that composes badly with P4.3?
  A9.5  MINER GRIEFING: at jackpot sizes, could a miner-walker throw
        away blocks to reroll D6.2? (At current sizes: absurd. State
        the bound at which it stops being absurd.)
  A9.6  Anything in §5's constants. All numbers are guesses. Attack
        with mechanism, not taste.
